Druids who worship Strahd as lord of the land and master of weather convene here, atop **Yester Hill**, on the edge of the demiplane

This place is where the evil blights walking the Svalich Woods are born, and where Strahd comes on occasion to glimpse his ancestral home 

Yester Hill gets its name from a strange phenomenon that can be observed by anyone who looks into the mists from the hilltop or the western hillside

###### Y1. Trail
>>The trail through the thick woods leads to a hill covered with dead grass and cairns of black rock. Dark, ominous clouds gather high above, and a single bolt of lightning strikes the hilltop. West of the hill, the land, the woods, and the sky vanish behind a towering wall of fog.

Trail splits as it climbs the hillside, forming two rings at the bottom to [[#Y2. Berserker Cairns]]

Trail also continues to the hilltop to [[#Y3. Druids' Circle]]
###### Y2. Berserker Cairns
>>Dirt trails run along two concentric rings of cairns that encircle the hillside. Each cairn is a ten-foot-high mound of slimy black rocks.

These burial mounds predate the **Druids** and Strahd, undisturbed for centuries
* In the cairns are moldy bones of an ancient tribe of berserkers that once lived in the mountain
###### Y3. Druids' Circle
>>Atop the hill is a wide ring of black boulders and smaller rocks that collectively form a makeshift wall enclosing a field of dead grass. Lightning strikes the edge of the ring from time to time, illuminating a ghastly, fifty-foot-tall statue made of tightly woven twigs and packed with black earth. The statue resembles a towering, cloaked man with fangs.

**Ring of boulders** is 250 ft diameter, 5-10 ft high
* PCs climbing the boulders have a **10% chance** of being struck by lightning for **8d10 lightning damage**
* The damage can be avoided by sticking to the two trails that pass through the ring

The **Giant Wooden Statue** bears an eerie resemblance to Strahd
* AC 10, 50 hp, immunity to psychic and poison damage
	* If splashed with oil and caught on fire, takes 2d6 fire damage each round
	* If reduced to 0 hp, statue collapses and contents tumble onto the field
* If the PCs approach or damage the statue, the **Druids** and **Berserkers** rise and attack
* The roots are part of the [[#Y4. Gulthias Tree]], wrapping around the statue for extra support and durability
	* The roots preven topplingg and allow the effigy to be a component for a druidic ritual to summon tree blights
* In the heart of the wooden effigy is one of the missing gems from the [[W. The Wizard of Wines]] vineyard. It glows with glowing bright green light
	* PCs using magic can learn the gem's location
	* Climbing the statue requires no ability check, and a PC near the chest can use an action to dig into it
		* Roll 1d20, 13 or higher and the PC unearths the gem and can take it


**Passive Wisdom 16 or higher** notices a dozen **covered graves**
* Six **Druids** and six **Berserkers** (CE Male/Female Humans) are buried on this hill and covered head to toe in blue-gray mud. They have long tangled hair and wild-looking eyes
	* They are descendants of the ancient mountain tribe whose members are buried on this hill
	* They honor their dark "god" by sleeping in earthen graves hidden under covers made of sod and dead grass
* If discovered, the **Druids** and **Berserkers** rise and attack
* **Druids** are waiting to begin a ritual to summon **Wintersplinter**, a huge **Tree Blight**
	* Won't begin without Strahd's blessing
	* They wait for Strahd to appear
	* Only one druid is needed
	* Cant be completed if the statue is destroyed or the magic gem is removed
* **If killed**, their numbers replenish from the Svalich Woods, **1d4 - 1 Druids** and **1d4 -1 Berserkers** arrive at dusk until there are **six of each**

###### Y5. Wall of Fog
>>As you look west into the curtain of fog, you see a white fortress on a hill above a great city. The city appears quite distant, maybe a mile away. The fog obscures all detail, but you can hear what sounds like the echo of a church bell.

Creatures entering fog suffer [[Mists of Ravenloft Effect]]s

**Strahd knows**:
* The Dark Powers have created a false image of Strahd's ancestral home within the fog, just beyond reach
* He comes to the hill occasionally to gaze upon the city (even though it he knows it isn't real)

**NPC Spellcasters know**:
* Ancient legend, according to the [[Barovians]] mountain folk, there was always a mist near Yester Hill, before the deadly mists trapped Barovia
* The ancient folk called the mist the "Whispering Wall", for within they could hear whispers of voices from the past and the future
* They believed an ancient god gave up his divinity to preserve the world from destruction, and that his last exhalation as a god produced this mist, within it all his memories of the world and all his visions of its possible futures
* With proper preparation, a seeker could go on a vision quest within the Whispering Wall.
* Some students of the arcane believe that the Dark Powers containing Strahd in Barovia took some of that fog to create the mists of Barovia, and that perhaps Strahd's domain is just a dark memory in the Whispering Wall.

##### Events
###### Druid's Ritual
(This can occur whether the characters visit Yester Hill. In such a case, PCs can still deal with Wintersplinter at [[W. The Wizard of Wines]])

[[Strahd von Zarovich]] travels to Yester Hill, either astride his nightmare, [[Beucephalus]] (if alive), or in bat form

After arriving, the **Druids** in [[#Y3. Druids' Circle]] "rise" from their graves to begin the ritual
* Ritual won't work if **Gulthias tree** is at 0 hp
	* Druids wont attempt ritual in this case
* Druids use their **actions** to chant and dance
* Druids must chant for **10 consecutive rounds**, **at least one** of them chanting
	* If a round goes by **without chanting**, the ritual has to **start over**
* **Strahd** **stays** to observe and **defend the Druids** if they are attacked

If the Druids complete the ritual:
>>A thirty-foot-tall plant creature bursts out of the statue, sending twigs and earth flying. The creature resembles a dead treant with green light seeping out of it.

A **Tree Blight** the Druids call "Wintersplinter" emerges from the wooden statue.
* The green light is the [[W. The Wizard of Wines]] **gem** in its "heart", and can only be **removed** when Wintersplinter is **dead**

The **Druids command** Wintersplinter, in **Druidic**, to travel north and **destroy** the Winery Vineyard
* **Following Wintersplinter** makes it clear to PCs that it is **going to the Winery**

Once Wintersplinter departs, Strahd commands the Druids and Berserkers to **leave the hill** to give him solitude
* Strahd gazes longingly at the image of his ancestral homeland in the [[#Y5. Wall of Fog]]
