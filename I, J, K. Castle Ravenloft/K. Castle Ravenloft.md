##### Random Encounters

Check for a random encounter:

- Every 10 minutes the characters spend resting in the castle.
- The first time the characters enter a castle area that isn't otherwise occupied

In most circumstances, a random encounter occurs on a roll of 18 or higher on a d20. To determine what the characters encounter, consult the table below.
