##### Overview
Run by [[Davian Martikov]], father of [[Urwin Martikov]]

Most of Barovia's wine comes from one source: The Wizard of Wines winery and vineyard

Founded by a mage whose name is lost to history. The wizard fashioned three magic gems, each the size of a pinecone, and planted them in the rich valley soil.

These "seeds" gave rise to healthy grapevines, which produced sweet, plump grapes. Even after Strahd's curse befell Barovia, the gems kept the vines and their grapes from succumbing to the darkness.

Strahd bequeathed the winery to the Krezkov family as a reward for the family's loyalty. Later, an arranged marriage between the Krezkovs and Martikovs led to the land being taken over by a Martikov descendent, who have tended the winery ever since

At some point, the Martikov family became infected with widespread lycanthropy. They are wereravens

The wereravens provide the wine to Barovian taverns for free, knowing the good it brings the Barovian people

###### Three Wines
Winery is known for three wines (lowest to highest quality):
* Purple Grapemash No. 3
* Red Dragon Crush
* Champagne du le Stomp

###### The First Missing Gem
Ten years ago, one of the [[W. The Wizard of Wines]] vineyard's magic gems was dug up and stolen, and as a result, the winery stopped producing its best vintage, the champagne. No one knows what happened to the gem. [[Davian Martikov]] blames his middle son, [[Urwin Martikov]], for the loss because Urwin was on watch the night the gem was taken. Davian is convinced that Urwin shirked his duty to spend time with his betrothed, and the two men have been at odds ever since. To this day, Urwin steadfastly denies his father's accusation.

###### Baba Lysaga's attacks (Second Missing Gem)
The Wereravens have been fending off attacks by [[Baba Lysaga]]'s scarecrows. Three weeks ago, during an attack, another gem was found, dug up, and taken

[[Davian Martikov]] believes [[Baba Lysaga]] possesses it, and he is correct

Baba still sends scarecrows against the winery, antagonizing the wereravens

###### Evil Druid Attacks (Third Missing Gem)
Five days ago, evil druids stole the third and final gem and bore it to [[Y. Yester Hill]]. The wereravens launched a counterattack on Yester Hill, but to no avail, they were no match for the druids

Two days ago, the druids returned with a horde of blights and drove Davian's family from the winery.

The druids also posioned the fermentation vats, leaving the winery with only a few bottles and barrels of wine

###### Dying Winery

Even if the characters help the Martikovs recover the winery, wine production in the valley will eventually stop as the vineyard dies off, unless the magic gems are recovered and replanted in the soil.

##### Aftermath

**If the winery is rid of its invaders:** 
* [[Davian Martikov]] is grateful and tells the PCs about the vineyard's three magic seeds" that have been stolen.

	"They are gems the size and shape of pinecones, each containing a glowing green light as bright as a torch. If you care for the good of all Barovians, I urge you to travel to retrieve the gems. [[Berez]] and [[Y. Yester Hill]] are the locations of two we still know of, though the third has been lost to time."

**If PCs ask for wine delivered to [[N2. Blue Water Inn]], [[N9. Vistani Camp]], or [[the burgomaster of Krezk]]:**
* [[Davian Martikov]], grateful to the PCs, sends his sons to the task immediately.
	* Adrian Martikov brings the three remaining barrels up from the cellar and secures them on the wagons, while Elvir Martikov secures the horses
	* Adrian and Elvir make the delivery themselves, but welcome the PCs escort
		* Davian suggest they go along to ensure the wagon's safety
	* If PCs escort wagon, roll for a random encounter for each mile traveled
		* The wagon is watched over by two **Swarms of Ravens** that attack anything that threatens the wagon or PCs
	* PCs can trade the six barrels for treasure from the [[Keepers of the Feather]] or Vistani, and can use it to buy their way into Krezk

**If PCs leave the winery and later return:**
* (If not destroyed) Wintersplinter, a **Tree Blight** from [[Y. Yester Hill]] us sent to ravage the vineyard and destroy the winery
* PCs return to find the grapevines trampled and the winery in ruins
* Wintersplinter's tracks are clearly visible on the trail to the south
	* PCs that follow the tracks catch up to Wintersplinter as the blight slowly goes back to [[Y. Yester Hill]]
* The Martikovs narrowly escape and flee to [[N2. Blue Water Inn]] in Vallaki
	* There [[Davian Martikov]] is crushed by the loss of the winery
* Morale in Vallaki sinks to an all-time low as word of the winery's destruction spreads through town
* **Three days after** this attack, [[Baba Lysaga]] sends seven **Scarecrows** from [[Berez]] and orders them to guard the vineyard to discourage the wereravens from returning
	* The **Scarecrows** attack anyone who crosses the vineyard or approaches the ruined winery
