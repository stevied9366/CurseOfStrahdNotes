N Human Scout
Location: [[N2. Blue Water Inn]]

Vallakian Hunter, kills wolves and sells meat for a living

Grim, haunted look in the eyes

Usually parrots [[Szoldar Szoldarovich]] in fewer words

Adds a new swatch to his wolfskin cloak for every wolf he’s killed

Rarely passes an opportunity to earn coin. 

Can be a guide or offer information about Barovia. 

Has a family but spend most of his time with [[Szoldar Szoldarovich]], either drinking or hunting

Most of the wolf heads that adorn [[N2. Blue Water Inn]]'s walls are their trophies
