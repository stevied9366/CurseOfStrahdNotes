###### **N2c. Taproom**

| Damp cloaks hang from pegs in the entrance portico. The tavern is packed with tables and chairs, with narrow paths meandering between them. A bar stretches along one wall, under a balcony that can be reached by a wooden staircase that hugs the north wall. Another balcony overhangs an entrance to the east. All the windows are fitted with thick shutters and crossbars. Lanterns hanging above the bar and resting on the tables bathe the room in dull orange light and cast shadows upon the walls, most of which are adorned with wolf heads mounted on wooden plaques. |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

Open after Noon
* From noon to dusk, the taproom usually has 2d4 patrons (commoners)
* Between dusk and midnight, 2d8 patrons, and an additional patron might be here 

Behind the bar, mounted on braces and tucked into alcoves, are three wine barrels, each ⅔ full, with a brass spigot in each of them
* Two barrels are Purple Grapemash No. 3 (cheap wine)
* The third is Red Dragon Crush (fine wine)

[[Danika Dorakova]] usually tends the bar

Scampering about are the Martikov boys, [[Brom and Bray Martikov]]

Windows and doors can be barred from within

Possible NPC Encounters:

* [[Szoldar Szoldarovich]] & [[Yevgeni Krushkin]]
	* Dour fellows, but rarely pass an opportunity to earn coin. They can be guides or offer information about Barovia. Both have families but spend most of their time together, either drinking or hunting
	* They’ll guide the PCs for 5 gp per day. They aren’t afraid to venture beyond Vallaki’s walls and know the woods & valley well
	* For a drink, they’ll give the PCs directions to important landmarks
	* They won’t travel “this cursed realm” at night for anything less than 100 gp
	* Most of the wolf heads that adorn the tavern walls are their trophies

  * [[Nikolai & Carl Wachter]]
	* Want to hear tales of PCs harrowing adventures or how the PCs plan to free Vallaki from the Burgomaster’s madness

* [[Rictavio]]
	* At dawn and dusk, Rictavio leaves the inn with some apples and cooked wolf steak wrapped in a handkerchief, claiming the food is for his portly friend “the destitute toymaker and his pet monkey ([[Blinsky]])
		* Actually, the apples are for his horse Drusilla, in [[#**N2f. Stable**]].
		* The steak is for his sabre-toothed tiger in [[N5. Arasek Stockyard]]
		* He isn't ready to unleash the tiger on the Vistani just yet. He feeds it by dropping wolf steaks down a 1-foot-square hatch in the wagon's roof. A character who climbs atop the wagon can spot the hatch without needing to make an ability check. 
	* Rictavio is gathering info on the Keepers of the Feather, trying to ID all the wereravens in town, and learning as much as possible about the Vistani, particularly the ones in the camp outside town ([[Vistani Camp]])
	* If he concludes PCs are in league with Strahd, he plans to unleash his Sabre-toothed tiger upon them, with or without the wereravens support

###### **N2n. Private Guest Room (Rictavio’s)**

Door is in plain view of taproom below

Room is locked at all times

Only [[Rictavio]], [[Urwin Martikov]] and [[Danika Dorakova]] carry keys

| This small guest room contains a bed heaped with wolf furs, a footlocker, a tall wardrobe, and a writing desk with matching chair. An oil lamp rests atop the desk near a journal bound in a red leather jacket. |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

Between midnight and dawn, Rictavio sleeps here

At dawn, Rictavio leaves the inn with some apples and cooked wolf steak wrapped in a handkerchief, claiming the food is for his portly friend “the destitute toymaker and his pet monkey

Noon to dusk, 40% chance he is here

At dusk Rictavio repeats his morning routine, then returns to retire for the night

Footlocker and wardrobe contain common clothes and travel wear

On the desk Rictavio seems to have left a journal

The journal tells of his exploits as an entertainer in search of new acts for his travelling carnival, making frequent mentions of conversations with Drusilla (no mention she is a horse) and recounts many long and tedious journeys by wagon. Rictavio has also noted various “oddities” he has seen, including:

- A “werehare” child, a boy who transforms into a rabbit on nights of the full moon
- A half-orc woman named Gorabacha who could chew through iron chains
- A giant, man-eating plant with the most remarkable singing voice
- A pair of conjoined goblins
- A small man with no legs named Filmore Stunk, who could drink whole casks of wine without getting drunk

	The journal is a lie to perpetuate the illusion that he is an entertainer
